Trustworthy AI Reading Group

Welcome to the Trustworthy AI Reading Group! This reading group is dedicated to exploring critical topics in AI, including fairness, security, explainability, and their broader applications. We aim to create an inclusive environment that fosters lively discussions and encourages participation from undergraduate, master’s, and PhD students alike.

Organizers

Dr. Sharon Levy, Department of Computer Science, Rutgers University

Dr. Ruixiang (Ryan) Tang, Department of Computer Science, Rutgers University

Meeting Details

When: Every Tuesday, 2:00 pm - 3:00 pm

Where: Hill Center, Room 482

First Session: October 22nd

Each session will include the presentation and discussion of one or two research papers. We look forward to your participation!

If you're interested in AI research or curious about the ethical and technical challenges of Trustworthy AI, we would love to have you join us. We highly encourage students to present their own work, ideas, or interesting papers. Please feel free to edit the CSV file [link] to select a time and paper you'd like to present and send an email to the organizers (s.levy@rutgers.edu, ruixiang.tang@rutgers.edu).

Feel free to share this with anyone who might be interested!
