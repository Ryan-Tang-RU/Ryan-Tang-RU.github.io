  • T1D Knowledge Graph Explorer

    2026 · in development

    knowledge graphliterature miningPubTator

    A temporally evolving knowledge graph of the type 1 diabetes literature - 131,511 PubMed records from 1960 to 2026, with 26,185 normalised entities and 262,510 extracted relations. The explorer shows co-mention neighbourhoods over ten time windows, the papers behind every edge, and Kleinberg burst intervals per entity. Built for the PanKbase AI Pilot with the TRAIL Lab at Rutgers.

    • Counts are shown as a share of the papers carrying an identified mention of the same entity type, not of the whole corpus, because annotation coverage roughly triples across the range and almost everything rises against a raw denominator.
    • MeSH indexing lags about four years, so the last few years are under-indexed rather than quiet.
    • PubTator's normalisation is occasionally wrong in ways worth knowing. The string NOD resolves to Myoclonic Epilepsies, and A1C is read as the coding substitution c.1A>C - the corpus's two largest "variants" are both that artifact.
